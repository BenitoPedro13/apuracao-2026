# TASK: read TSE `dt/ht` in the municipality's local time

Must-have correctness fix before the night (research 03 §2). Small in code, careful in
data.

## 1. Current scenario

`tseInstant(dt, ht)` (`packages/contracts/src/tse/primitives.ts`) appends `-03:00` to every
TSE date/time. Research 03 §2 showed, against the TSE's own BU files, that the result
feed's `dt/ht` are in the **municipality's local time**: Acre's coverage rows are ~2 h
behind Brasília (median +116.8 min from the last BU to the row), Amazonas ~1 h (+59.8,
with a western group at ~2 h), DF ~0 (−10.5). So every instant we derive from a
municipal or coverage row in a UTC−4/−5 municipality (AC, AM, RO, RR, MT, MS) or UTC−2
(Fernando de Noronha) is off by 1–2 h, and abroad rows are presumably each city's local
time.

Who reads those instants:

- **Recorder, tier-2 trigger:** compares a coverage row's instant with the same
  municipality's file instant. Both are read the same way, so the comparison is right.
  **Not affected**; it changes nothing on the night if left alone.
- **Views** (`packages/views`): `totalizedAt` / `generatedAt` shown as "TSE: hh:mm", and
  anything that orders or compares instants across municipalities or UFs (the timeline).
- **fake-tse:** the reveal order (Acre's municipalities appear 2 h early in a replay).
- **The pointer's `tse` stamps** come from the br file: correct only if the br file is in
  Brasília time `[VERIFY: §2.1]`.

Two signals per municipality, from the 2026 1st round's BU files, already disagree in
places (research 03; AM, 2026-10-08):

- *last BU received (Brasília) − row `ht`*, rounded to hours: 11 western AM municipalities
  at +2 h (Guajará, Atalaia do Norte, Benjamin Constant, Eirunepé, Envira, Ipixuna, Jutaí,
  São Paulo de Olivença, Amaturá, Itamarati, Tabatinga);
- *median local urn closing time* (`DT_ENCERRAMENTO`; polls close at 17:00 Brasília
  everywhere, so 15h local = UTC−5, 16h = UTC−4, 17h = UTC−3): the same 10 except São
  Paulo de Olivença (16h), and 8 AM municipalities at 17h, which no single offset explains
  (late closings, or urn clocks) `[VERIFY]`.

## 2. Planned changes

### 2.1 Resolve the facts first (research 03 §2, with samples)

1. **Per-municipality offset table** for every municipality with a result file (5,571 +
   186 abroad): download the 28 BU files of the 2026 1st round (sha512-checked), compute
   both signals per municipality, and accept an offset only where they agree; where they
   don't, decide from a third source, the legal time zones: Lei 12.876/2013 for AC and
   western AM and the IANA tz database zones (`America/Rio_Branco`, `America/Eirunepe`,
   `America/Manaus`, `America/Porto_Velho`, `America/Boa_Vista`, `America/Cuiaba`,
   `America/Campo_Grande`, `America/Noronha`), cited per municipality. Abroad: each
   city's offset on 2026-10-04 from IANA (DST included), checked against the ZZ BU file.
2. **UF, br and zz rows/files:** is the br `-ab`'s UF row for AC in Acre time, and is the
   br `-u` file's `dt/ht` in Brasília time? Check against the last BU received per UF and
   nationally.
3. **2nd round** (`6258`/`6260`): same municipalities, same rule `[VERIFY on the first
   2nd-round files, from 10-20]`.

The table is committed as data: `packages/tse/data/utc-offsets.json` (TSE municipality
code → minutes, with the source of each entry), generated by
`scripts/derive-utc-offsets.ts` from the BU files, reproducible.

### 2.2 Code

- `packages/contracts`: `tseInstant(dt, ht, offsetMinutes = -180)`; the default keeps
  Brasília for files that are national.
- `packages/tse`: `utcOffsetOf(area, mu?)` from the table.
- `packages/views` (`extractResult`, `extractCoverage`): the municipality's (or UF's)
  offset. View instants become correct ISO instants with their real offset.
- `apps/fake-tse` (`reveal.ts`): the same, so replays reveal in real order.
- **The recorder is left alone** (correct comparisons, no reason to redeploy it for this).

### 2.3 Rejected

- *A per-UF offset only:* wrong for western Amazonas (≥ 10 municipalities on UTC−5), and
  Fernando de Noronha.
- *Inferring offsets at run time from the BU files:* the BUs of the 2nd round don't exist
  during the night; the table must exist before it.

## 3. Why

On the night the site shows "TSE: hh:mm" per state and municipality, and the timeline
orders results across states. Off by 2 h for Acre is a wrong number on screen (invariant 1
in spirit). Cost: ~0.5 day; the BU downloads are public, a few GB once.

## 6. Outcome (2026-10-08)

- **Table:** `packages/tse/src/data/utc-offsets.json` (80 KB), all **5,757** municipalities:
  5,198 at UTC−3, 339 at UTC−4, 33 at UTC−5, 1 at UTC−2 (Fernando de Noronha, `30015`),
  186 abroad by IANA zone; `byUf` from each UF's capital (AC −5; AM, MT, MS, RO, RR −4; the
  rest −3). Generated by `scripts/derive-utc-offsets.ts` from the 28 BU zips of the 2026
  1st round (`scripts/fetch-bweb.ts`, 5.08 GB, every sha512 checked).
- **Method as built** (changed from §2.1): the urns' opening time (08:00 Brasília) is the
  value and the BU receipts a lower bound: a row re-stamped later fits smaller shifts too
  (Maceió, Manaus). 6 municipalities where the two conflict (3 MT, 3 PR) and Rabat take the
  legal zone (IANA tzdb 2026e `zone1970.tab`), cited in `notes`. Abroad, each city is
  mapped to its IANA zone by name (`scripts/abroad-zones.json`) and checked against BU
  receipts (≤ 30 min); offsets are computed at each stamp's date, because Europe leaves
  summer time on 2026-10-25, the 2nd round's day.
- **What's local and what isn't** (research 03 §2): `dg/hg` are Brasília; municipal rows
  and files' `dt/ht` are local, except the TSE's central re-stamp (05/10 12:51:05), which
  is Brasília and is told apart because a local reading would fall after the file's own
  generation (exactly the 372 such files of UTC−4/−5 UFs). A UF-level stamp copies the
  wall clock of its latest municipality (the UF's zone; western Amazonas can make AM's 1 h
  off); `br` and its UF rows' own re-stamps are Brasília.
- **pe30015 explained:** its row (17:57:45) is Noronha time, i.e. 16:57:45 Brasília,
  before the `-ab`'s generation. The "row 58 min after its file" in `TASK-fake-tse.md`
  §2.2 was this time zone; the min(row, own `hg`) rule stays as a safeguard.
- **Verification:** median last-BU → row lag per UF now 0.4–3.5 min (was +116.8 for AC,
  +59.8 for AM); the replay's first Acre file appears at 18:17 Brasília (was 16:17, before
  polls closed) and only 1 event precedes 17:00 Brasília (was 25) `[VERIFY: which]`;
  `pnpm turbo run lint check-types test build` 34/34 (tse 63 tests; the views
  determinism test needed a cached `Intl.DateTimeFormat` per zone to stay under 5 s).
  The recorder is unchanged and not redeployed.
